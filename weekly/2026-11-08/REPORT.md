**The repaired binary64 sine passes its full pinned worst-case corpus under Clang in all four rounding modes. The near-one log stopping test has a new certificate covering three evaluation orders, and eight below-midpoint hypot pairs passed on GCC and Clang.** No new wrong value or compiler defect was demonstrated.

1. **Log: a proof model that matches what GCC emits.** With fast contraction on x86-64-v3, GCC 13.3 fuses the product into *both* operations of the high/low split. The supplied Gappa file rounds that product first. The [companion proof and reproducer](LOG.md) covers both orders and the fully uncontracted order. Two rational enclosures certify the real polynomial error below `2^-70 |x−1|`. Gappa bounds and an exact margin check leave enough room in all four modes for the near-one early return. The tighter endpoint bound quoted in the source was unnecessary and did not close in two toward-zero replays. This is a value proof for the stated arithmetic graphs; it does not prove the fallback, flags or all of log.
