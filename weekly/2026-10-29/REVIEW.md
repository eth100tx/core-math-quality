# Independent review disposition

Actual Claude Opus 5.5 CLI (`claude-opus-5-5`) supplied independent planning,
workflow, final substantive and correction-confirmation reviews. The reviewer
read supplied code/proof excerpts, complete proposed patch, checker sources,
receipts and intended claims; it did not execute tests or independently fetch
the repositories. The operator separately ran the reported checks and verified
source bindings. Private full review records are retained outside this repository.

The first final review held publication for ambiguous sign prose, the reducer
distance term, corpus/generator wording, an outdated classification log and the
guard completeness premise. These were resolved with explicit four-case algebra,
the reduction-error term, observed corpus classes, matching 256/512-bit logs and
an explicit unclosed hard-case premise. The follow-up verdict was PASS conditional
on an explicit final sign formula and three source/evidence checks. Those
conditions were verified before publication:

- Final sign is `b xor q xor d`; C applies `sbit xor (i1 >= 32)` in `u128_tod`,
  after the difference comparison updates `sbit`. The patch names algorithm
  line `accline17a`. All 24 exact sign cases pass, including equality.
- The scanner uses `0x1.fffffffffffffp-1 = 1-2^-53` for the near-unity class.
  Its minimum update is after the small-cosine filter, so the quoted minimum
  belongs to that subset. The two current precision logs match CLASSIFICATION.
- The patch applies cleanly to the exact proof base and yields the recorded
  candidate bytes. The final hashes cover the current evidence files.

Review suggestions were checked, not treated as authority: the sine reducer also
uses index -1 (with different shifts), and an earlier suggested sign condition
covered only one quadrant. Requiring no other input ever to hit the exception
guard would be stronger than needed; the actual unresolved premise is no further
input *needing correction*. None of these suggestions is reported as a new bug.

The workflow review's encoding, finite-budget and Sunday-ordering conditions were
also resolved. This review does not turn finite tests into a whole-function proof,
establish exhaustive census completeness or prove fallback unreachability.
